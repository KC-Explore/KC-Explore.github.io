You have a laptop. Maybe a spare PC gathering dust. And a nagging sense that
"running your own AI" is something other people do — people with server racks, a
CS degree, and a tolerance for YAML.

It isn't. I started with one machine and a lot of wrong turns, and this is the
guide I wish I'd had. It's first-person on purpose: I'll tell you what I actually
run, what broke, and what I'd skip if I started over — and the boxed *"How I run
mine"* sidebars are my real setup, scrubbed of anything that would help a stranger
find it. No hype. Just the path from zero to a homelab you'll actually use.
