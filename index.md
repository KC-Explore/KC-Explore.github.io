# Start Your AI Homelab

You have a laptop. Maybe a spare PC gathering dust. And a nagging sense that
"running your own AI" is something other people do — people with server racks,
a CS degree, and a tolerance for YAML.

It isn't. I started with one machine and a lot of wrong turns, and this series is
the guide I wish I'd had. It's written first-person on purpose: I'll tell you what
I actually run, what broke, and what I'd skip if I started over. The core of each
post is generic enough to follow on whatever hardware you've got — the boxed
**"How I run mine"** sidebars are just my real setup, scrubbed of anything that
would help a stranger find it.

No hype, no "AI will change everything" filler. Just the path from zero to a
homelab you'll actually use.
